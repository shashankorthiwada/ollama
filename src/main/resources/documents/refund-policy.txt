Banking Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund for eligible card transactions.

Refund requests must be submitted within 30 days of the original transaction.

Once a refund is approved, the amount will normally be credited
to the customer's account within 5 to 7 business days.

Some refunds may take longer depending on the merchant and payment network.

Customers can contact customer support if the refund has not been
received after 7 business days.
